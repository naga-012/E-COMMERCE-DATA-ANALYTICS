## 4. Elevator Pitch / 60-Second Interview Explanation

> "In this project, I served as the lead data analyst for a simulated multi-million rupee Indian e-commerce marketplace operating across 2022 to 2024. Starting with raw, uncurated transactional feeds with typical real-world flaws, I engineered a Python cleaning pipeline to audit 52,500 orders and 5,156 customer accounts. 
> 
> I used SQL and Python to answer critical business questions: who are our most profitable customers, why are returns happening, and which categories actually make money? 
> 
> My analysis uncovered that while Electronics accounted for 40% of our ₹ 13.5 Crore top-line, it only yielded a 17.5% margin, while categories like Accessories generated over 50% net profit. I also identified 584 high-spending customers at risk of churn, and tracked reverse logistics where sizing issues caused 20.9% of all returns. 
> 
> I translated these findings into an interactive 4-page Power BI dashboard and an Excel model, delivering actionable recommendations to recover ₹ 5 Crore in at-risk revenue and lower return rates."
